## 4. Method caveat

A strict pattern match finds **zero** 山經 water-names in 海經, because
海經 writes 「X水出Y」 where 山經 writes 「X水出焉」. That is a method
artefact, not a fact: the rivers *are* shared (渭, 洛, 涇, 漢, 濟, 汝,
漳, 汾, 沁, 弱水, 黑水, 赤水, 洋水, 甘水 all appear in both). They were
checked by name, not by pattern.

Name overlap is also weak evidence on its own: a shared name can mean a
shared place, a shared name-stock, or a later editor harmonising the two
texts. Only §2a and §2b give a join that can be drawn on a map.
