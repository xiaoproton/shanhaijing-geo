# Audit — 中次十經


Per-entry record of **what was excluded** and why. This is the safety net: it lets anyone see every character dropped, and challenge the rule.

Rule applied: *include a clause if it asserts a spatial relation between two named places.* Everything below asserts no spatial relation.

Source: zh.wikisource, `山海經/中山經` (郭璞注 removed).

---

### Entry 1 — 首陽之山

**Kept:** `《中次十經》之首，曰首陽之山`

**Excluded:**
> 其上多金玉，無草木

*flora · minerals*

### Entry 2 — 虎尾之山

**Kept:** `又西五十里，曰虎尾之山`

**Excluded:**
> 其木多椒椐，多封石；其陽多赤金，其陰多鐵

*flora · minerals*

### Entry 3 — 繁繢之山

**Kept:** `又西南五十里，曰繁繢之山`

**Excluded:**
> 其木多楢杻，其草多枝勾

*flora*

### Entry 4 — 勇石之山

**Kept:** `又西南二十里，曰勇石之山`

**Excluded:**
> 無草木，多白金，多水

*flora · minerals*

### Entry 5 — 復州之山

**Kept:** `又西二十里，曰復州之山`

**Excluded:**
> 其木多檀，其陽多黃金。有鳥焉，其狀如鴞，而一足彘尾，其名曰跂踵，見則其國大疫

*flora · fauna · minerals*

### Entry 6 — 楮山

**Excluded:**

### Entry 7 — 又原之山

**Excluded:**

**Excluded:**

### Entry 9 — 丙山

### 小結

**Excluded:**
